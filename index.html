Shopify Payments

Shopify Payments is the simplest way to accept payments online. It's built directly into Shopify — no separate merchant account, no third-party gateway setup. When you create your store, you're automatically ready to accept all major payment methods.

Conversion Features

Shopify Payments unlocks native checkout features that meaningfully move conversion — none of which are available with third-party gateways.

Digital Wallets

Native support for Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Shop Pay right out of the box — zero additional integration work. Meets customers exactly where they want to pay.

Overview ↗

Shop Pay

150M+ customers with saved payment info. 1-click checkout, up to 50% higher conversion vs. guest checkout. Shop Pay's baseline lift alone is 5%.

Shop Pay Installments

BNPL that increases AOV by 30–50% and conversion by 20%+. Lets customers split a $400 purchase into manageable payments at no cost to them.

70+ Local Payment Methods

iDEAL, Klarna, Afterpay, ACH, and more. Selling internationally without local methods costs 20–30% in conversion. ACH covers high-value and wholesale orders.

Dynamic Checkout Optimization

Shopify constantly A/B tests and improves its checkout. Every merchant benefits automatically — no engineering work required.

Shopify Audiences Plus Exclusive

Shopify Audiences generates custom audience lists of high-intent buyers in the US and Canada and automatically exports them to your ad platforms (Meta, Google, etc.). Audiences are matched to your specific business model — reaching prospective customers most likely to buy from you, not just generic interest targeting. Requires Shopify Payments to be active.

Overview ↗

Shopify Balance & Capital

Access to financial products tied directly to your store performance. Shopify Balance provides a business account with cashback rewards, and Shopify Capital offers funding based on your sales history — no lengthy applications or credit checks. Only available to merchants on Shopify Payments.

Shop Pay Installments

Shop Pay Installments is Shopify's built-in BNPL (Buy Now, Pay Later) solution. It lets customers split purchases into 4 interest-free payments or monthly installments — at no cost to them and no extra integration work for you. It increases AOV by 30–50% and conversion by 20%+, particularly for higher-ticket items.

Overview ↗

Key Stats
  • AOV increase: 30–50%
  • Conversion increase: 20%+
  • No interest for buyers
  • Available on orders $50–$17,500
  • No additional integration required

Unified Financial Reports

Payouts, revenue, refunds, and reconciliation all live in one dashboard. Eliminates the back-and-forth between payment processors, spreadsheets, and accounting tools — saving your finance team hours every week.

Real-Time Fraud Analysis

Every order is automatically scored for risk. Shopify provides specific recommendations that protect you from fulfilling fraudulent orders before they become chargebacks — no manual review queue required.

Chargeback Automation

Shopify Payments automatically handles dispute submissions using order data. Saves your team hours of manual work per chargeback — evidence is gathered and submitted on your behalf.
