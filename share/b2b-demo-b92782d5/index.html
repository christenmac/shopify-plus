Wholesale cart
B2B pricing carries through the cart — retail prices never appear for a logged-in company
🔒 downtown-auto.myshopify.com/cart
Why this matters: the cart uses the customer's company-specific catalog and price list. Quantity rules (minimums & increments) and volume tiers are enforced here, and the wholesale discount is already baked into every line — no coupon codes, no retail leakage.
←
B2B checkout
Net terms, pay-by-invoice, PO numbers, and tax-exempt resale — the wholesale checkout retail can't do
🔒 downtown-auto.myshopify.com/checkout
The B2B difference: checkout offers Net 30 pay-by-invoice alongside cards, captures a PO number, and applies the company's resale tax exemption automatically — all native to Shopify, no third-party wholesale app.
←
Order confirmed
Order placed on wholesale terms — invoice issued, no payment collected up front
🔒 downtown-auto.myshopify.com/orders
Full loop: the order flows straight into the merchant's Shopify admin as a B2B order tied to the company, with the invoice on Net 30 terms — the same order you'd see in the Orders and Draft orders screens of this demo.
←
Apply for wholesale
The public storefront form new buyers fill out — applications land in the admin as a Pending approval company
🔒 downtown-auto.myshopify.com/pages/wholesale
How this works: the storefront hosts a public Apply for wholesale page. On submit, Shopify creates a company in Pending approval — the buyer can't order until a rep reviews the resale certificate and assigns a price list & payment terms. Approved buyers then log in and see the wholesale portal.
←
Storefront home
The store a logged-in wholesale buyer lands on — same theme as retail, but wholesale pricing throughout
🔒 downtown-auto.myshopify.com
One storefront, two audiences: the same Shopify theme serves retail shoppers and B2B buyers. When a company buyer is signed in, Shopify swaps in their price list, catalog, and terms — the hero, nav, and best-sellers all show wholesale pricing automatically.
←
Wholesale catalog
The buyer only ever sees the products in their company catalog — priced on their price list
🔒 /collections/wholesale
Trade is prebuilt with the advanced B2B features: a quick order list for bulk ordering by SKU, volume pricing and quantity rules enforced on the product page, and an account request form for new customer sign-ups. Catalog membership, price list and currency all follow the company profile — swap companies and this page changes completely.
←
Orders & invoices — buyer self-serve
What the wholesale buyer sees in their own account, 24/7, without emailing your team
🔒 /account/orders
Self-serve is intuitive for your team and customers. Free up valuable resources by empowering your wholesale customers to manage their own orders and account information — 24/7 and from anywhere in the world. Order status, PO numbers, invoices, reorders and company contacts all live here.
←
Theme editor — B2B context
One store, one theme — content personalized by customer type and by market
You can personalize store content and promotions based on the type of customer you're selling to, or the market your customer is buying from — including language and currency, from a single store. Every section marked ● is overridden for the B2B context only; retail shoppers never see it.
