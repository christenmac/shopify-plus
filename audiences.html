How It Works
Your ad audiences, powered by
Shopify's entire buyer network.

Shopify Audiences pools anonymized, opt-in purchase signals from across millions of Shopify merchants — then uses machine learning to build audience lists specifically tuned to your store and products.

1
Connect your ad platforms
Link Meta, Google, TikTok, Pinterest, Snapchat, or Criteo directly from the Audiences app in just a few clicks. No developer needed.
2
Audiences are auto-generated
Within 24 hours, Shopify's ML model analyzes purchase intent signals across the network and builds custom lists matched to your catalog and customer profile.
3
Target high-intent buyers
Use Prospecting lists to find net-new customers who look like your best buyers — using data no ad platform has on its own.
4
Retarget & exclude smarter
Retargeting Boost lists increase retargeting conversions. Existing Customers Plus exclusion lists automatically prevent showing ads to people who already bought — cutting wasted spend by 40%+.
