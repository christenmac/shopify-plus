About this app
Shopify Audiences uses machine learning and purchase intent signals from across the Shopify merchant network to build high-converting custom audiences — automatically synced to Meta, Google, TikTok, Pinterest, Snapchat, and Criteo.

Merchants see up to 50% lower customer acquisition costs and 2x more orders per marketing dollar. Existing Customers Plus audiences exclude 40% more of your existing customers than standard ad platform lists — so your budget reaches net-new buyers.

Free and exclusive to Shopify Plus merchants using Shopify Payments in the US or Canada.
