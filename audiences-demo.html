About this app
Shopify Audiences uses ML and purchase signals from across the Shopify merchant network to build high-converting ad audiences — automatically synced to Meta, Google, TikTok, Pinterest, Snapchat, and Criteo.

Free and exclusive to Shopify Plus merchants using Shopify Payments in the US or Canada.
