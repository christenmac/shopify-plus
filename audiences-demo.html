Data sharing
Shopify Network Intelligence
To generate audiences, Shopify pools anonymized, opt-in purchase signals from participating merchants. Your customer data is hashed before entering the model — it is never shared with other merchants or ad platforms.
🔒
Privacy-safe by design: All data is hashed and aggregated before use. Individual customer records are never exposed. Customers can opt out at any time. Shopify is SOC 2 compliant.
⚖️
Legal compliance: Ensure your store complies with applicable US state and regional privacy laws before enabling data sharing.
